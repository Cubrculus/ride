Bearing from coordinates, radius from the listed distance. Schematic, not survey-accurate — use it to read cluster shape, not to drive by.

How the tiers work. A/B/C is inferred from structural proxies — room count, shuttle coverage, service class and distance — not from observed surge data. A hotel with a 24/7 free shuttle absorbs its own airport runs; one whose shuttle sleeps 22:00–05:00 hands those trips to you. Log what actually happens and re-rank.

Addresses. Navigation resolves by hotel name and city rather than by the stored street address, so an imprecise address never misroutes you. Rows marked addr unverified have a street number that could not be confirmed — the name is still correct.

Season. Everything south of Sarasota is heavily seasonal (peak Jan–Apr, hollow Jun–Sep). APF has no scheduled airline service; PGD is Allegiant-only.
